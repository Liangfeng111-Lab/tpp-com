About tpp.com

Your trusted destination for the best daily deals and savings

Our Story

tpp.com was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: help everyday people save money on the things they love. What started as a small blog sharing deals with friends and family has grown into one of the most trusted deal destinations on the web, reaching millions of savvy shoppers every month.

We believe that everyone deserves to get the best price — whether you're shopping for groceries, electronics, home goods, fashion, or anything in between. Our team scours the internet daily to find, verify, and share the most incredible bargains from your favorite stores.

How We Find Deals

Every deal on tpp.com is hand-picked by our editorial team. We don't use automated bots — real people find, verify, and curate every single deal. For Amazon deals in particular, we have strict quality standards: products must have 40+ positive reviews, a 4-star or higher rating, and pass our authenticity checks. We only share deals we would recommend to our own families.

Our team monitors hundreds of retailers, coupon databases, flash sale sites, and price trackers 24/7. When we spot a genuine bargain, we verify the price history, check for hidden fees, and confirm the coupon codes actually work — before it ever appears on our site.

How We Make Money

tpp.com is completely free for readers. We earn a small commission from some retailers when you make a purchase through our links — at no extra cost to you. This never influences which deals we feature. Our editorial integrity is our most valuable asset, and we take it seriously. If a deal isn't genuinely great, we don't post it — period.

Join Our Community

tpp.com is more than just a deal site — it's a community of smart shoppers helping each other save. Submit deals you find (we'll credit you!), save your favorites to your HipList, share tips in the comments, and follow us on social media for real-time deal alerts.

Have a question, suggestion, or just want to say hi? We'd love to hear from you. Reach out through our contact page or connect with us on social media.
